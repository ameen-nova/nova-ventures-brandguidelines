# AGENTS.md

This folder is the Nova Ventures brand kit: the rules, files and direction for making anything for the brand, from posters and social posts to decks, web pages, motion graphics and video.

## Read first

1. **`BRAND.md`**: the brand book and single source of truth. Read it in full before making anything.
2. `index.html`: the same guide, designed for people. Open it to see examples; don't treat its layouts as templates.

If `BRAND.md` and `index.html` disagree, `BRAND.md` wins.

## How to work

1. **Start from the idea.** Find the one thing the piece has to say and pick a way in (BRAND.md section 10).
2. **Build from the real files.** Place the logo from `Nova Ventures Logo Package/`, set type in the fonts from `Fonts/`, use colours from BRAND.md section 3, and draw shards from the geometry in section 6 or the files in `06 Shard/`. Never redraw or retype the logo.
3. **Uploading to a chat tool?** Upload `BRAND.md` and one logo PNG for your background. Not the whole folder, and not the originals in `svg/` or the root PNGs.
4. **Generate only photos and footage.** If an image or video model is involved, it makes the photograph or the clip. The logo, type, shards and layout are assembled on top.
5. **Check before delivering.** Run the checklist in BRAND.md section 14, and look at the rendered result, not just the code.

## Don't

- Don't use files marked "Proposed" (`07 Flat Lockups (Proposed)`, `NovaVentures_Symbol_FlatBlue`).
- Don't invent facts, figures, quotes or contact details. Leave a `[placeholder]`.
- Don't edit the logo files, fonts or `BRAND.md` unless you're asked to.

## Changing the brand

When a rule changes, update `BRAND.md` first, then update `index.html` to match.
