# Nova Ventures Brand Book

Version 1.0 · October 2026

This is the single source of truth for the Nova Ventures brand. It is written to be read by people and by AI tools. Use it to make anything: posters, social posts, carousels, presentations, documents, web pages, motion graphics and video.

`index.html` in this folder is the visual version of the same guide, for people. If the two ever disagree, this file wins, and the HTML should be updated to match. The guide's own interface (its side menu, ✓ and ✕ lists, swatch cards) is not a creative style to copy.

**How to read it:**
- Sections 1 to 9 describe the identity: who Nova is, how it sounds and how it looks. Treat these as fixed.
- Section 10 is creative direction. Read it as encouragement to explore, not as a template.
- Sections 11 to 13 cover motion, video and how to work with each kind of tool.
- Section 14 is the checklist to run before delivering anything.

## 2. Voice and copy

Nova sounds like an expert who makes things clear: knowledgeable, professional, concise, reassuring and action-oriented.

**Four voice pillars, each with a limit**
- **Clear, never complicated.** Familiar words, short sentences. Explain terms like "free zone", "trade licence" and "residency visa".
  Say: "Choose the right business setup for your needs." Not: "Optimise your jurisdictional incorporation strategy."
- **Confident, never boastful.** Show expertise through useful information and claims that can be supported.
  Say: "Our team will guide you through the setup process." Not: "The undisputed number one consultants in the UAE."
- **Supportive, never overfamiliar.** Professional and approachable, no slang.
  Say: "We're here to help you understand your options." Not: "Hey! Let's get your dream business sorted, buddy!"
- **Solution-focused, never pushy.** Outcomes and next steps, no pressure.
  Say: "Explore the package that suits your business." Not: "Sign up today or miss your only chance!"

**Five habits:** one idea per sentence, active voice · benefit first, technical detail second · explain terms · say what the client provides, what Nova handles and what depends on an authority · no false promises.

**Specific beats generic.** "Business beyond borders" is internal. In public copy, lead with the concrete line: name the place, the task or the next step. If a competitor could publish the sentence unchanged, rewrite it.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Unlock your global business potential. | Setting up your business in the UAE shouldn't be complicated. |
| Your gateway to global opportunities. | From your first licence to your next stage of growth. |
| Seamless, hassle-free solutions. | We tell you which documents you need, and we handle the submissions. |

**Words**

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Help, guide, support | Facilitate, leverage |
| Business setup | Unexplained incorporation jargon |
| Available options | Best option for everyone |
| Clear pricing | Cheapest in the UAE |
| Estimated processing time | Guaranteed approval time |
| Speak to our team | Unlock your potential today |

**Calls to action:** Speak to an advisor · Explore packages · Get started · Understand your options. Sentence case.

**Earn every claim.** Never publish a number (years in business, companies formed, clients served) until it is verified. No guaranteed approvals, fixed timings, universal eligibility or "lowest price". Testimonials only with the client's name, business and permission.

**Final test before publishing:** Is it easy to understand on first read? Does it answer a real client question? Knowledgeable without sounding superior? Does it say what to do next? Are the claims accurate? Would an international client new to the UAE understand it?

### Hot Pink: it marks the point

Pink has one job. It marks a point that matters, always as a small mark, never as an area. Visible enough to create distinction, restrained enough to never overpower the brand.

1. **The start**: the short dash (2 to 3 px) under a label, eyebrow or tagline; chapter and section openers.
2. **Where you are**: the active menu item, the current step, the current carousel dot or slide number.
3. **What matters**: the headline highlight now and then (instead of blue), the one figure or chart value that matters, one supporting shard (never the largest).
4. **The finish**: sign-offs, a card edge, a letterhead foot, an envelope flap, a folder spine, a notebook elastic.

**How much:** up to two pink moments in a layout, each doing a different job. Many pieces need one. If pink is the biggest thing or the first thing you notice, use less. If a pink element does none of the four jobs, remove it.

**Never in:** the logo · buttons, links, body text, icons · backgrounds, large shapes, photography · gradients, or mixed with blue inside one shard.

**Where it goes, by touchpoint**

| Touchpoint | Pink |
|---|---|
| Website | Dash under section labels, active menu underline, current step in "how it works" and form progress, one shard in the hero |
| Social | Dash under the label, current carousel dot or slide number, the headline highlight sometimes, one small shard |
| Presentations | Dash on the title slide, section number or progress marker, the key value in a chart |
| Stationery | Card edge or dash under the name, letterhead foot, envelope flap, folder spine |
| Merchandise | Elastic, ribbon bookmark, lanyard lining, clips. Small items can be fully pink; larger ones get a pink detail only |
| Office and signage | A thin line in wayfinding, one detail at reception |
| Motion and video | The dash drawing in, the current step marker, a dash on the end card |

## 4. Typography

**Arsenal Regular (400)** for display, H1 and H2: headlines, statements and pull quotes, at 28 px and above.
- What it looks like: a light, humanist **sans-serif** with gently tapered strokes, open shapes and a slightly condensed width. It is not a serif. Never replace it with Times, Georgia, Playfair or any other serif.
- Never Arsenal Bold. Italic only for pull quotes.
- Sentence case, never all caps. Line height 1.03 to 1.18. About −1% tracking at display sizes.

**Lato** for everything else: Regular (400) for body and lead text, Bold (700) for H3 and smaller headings, buttons and labels. A warm, rounded sans-serif.

**Highlight exactly one phrase per headline** in Nova Blue (Light Blue on dark), or now and then in Hot Pink. Same weight as the rest of the headline, never bold.

**Web scale**

| Style | Spec |
|---|---|
| Display | Arsenal Regular 64/66 px (mobile 40/44) |
| H1 | Arsenal Regular 48/52 px (mobile 34/38) |
| H2 | Arsenal Regular 34/40 px (mobile 28/34) |
| H3 | Lato Bold 22/30 px |
| Lead | Lato Regular 19/30 px, Slate |
| Body | Lato Regular 16/26 px, Charcoal or Slate |
| Small | Lato Regular 14/21 px |
| Label | Lato Bold caps 12 px, +200 tracking |
| Button | Lato Bold 15 px, 48 px tall |

Left-align. Centre only short, single statements. Body text 50 to 75 characters per line, 16 px minimum on screen, 9 pt in print.

**Fallback** where the fonts can't load: Trebuchet MS for headlines, Arial for text. In an AI tool that can't use the real fonts, choose the closest light humanist sans for headlines and say so.

**Font files:** `Fonts/Arsenal/Arsenal-Regular.ttf`, `Fonts/Arsenal/Arsenal-Italic.ttf`, `Fonts/Lato/Lato-Regular.ttf`, `Fonts/Lato/Lato-Bold.ttf`, `Fonts/Lato/Lato-Italic.ttf`. Both are free under the Open Font License.

## 5. Logo

A faceted blue-and-silver star with the NOVA VENTURES wordmark. The dimensional (metallic) logo is the only rendered element in the brand; everything else stays flat, apart from one glass element: a glass panel over a photograph (section 7) or a glass shard on a background that isn't white (section 6).

**Always place the supplied file.** Never redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rearrange, or add shadow, glow, outline or bevel. Never put it in a box. Never add a tagline, divider or text beside it, and never set "Business beyond borders" under it.

**Which version**

| Background | File |
|---|---|
| White or light | FullColour |
| Navy, Midnight, dark photo | Reversed |
| Nova Blue, or photography | MonoWhite |
| Grey, or one-colour print | MonoBlack |
| Busy imagery | Symbol only, sized generously |

On white, Cloud or Light Grey gives the silver sweep more contrast than pure white.

**Size in creatives:** the logo signs off, it doesn't lead. About **10% of the canvas width** (108 px on a 1080 px post), on its own, in a corner or along an edge. If 10% falls below the minimum sizes, use the vertical lockup or the symbol, or go slightly larger.

**Clear space:** x = the height of the N in NOVA, on every side.

**Minimum width:** horizontal 170 px / 40 mm · vertical 100 px / 25 mm · symbol 48 px / 8 mm. For favicons and app icons use the files in `05 Digital`.

**Lockups:** horizontal (default) · vertical (square layouts, limited width) · symbol (avatars, icons) · wordmark (only when the symbol appears elsewhere).

## 6. Graphics: the Shard System

One shape, drawn from the star's sweep: a slim, asymmetric, faceted blade. It stands for the separate pieces of a business journey (formation, licensing, visas, compliance, support) coming together and moving in one direction. **Different elements. One clear direction.**

**Geometry** (length 100 : width 18, centre origin, pointing right):
```
whole shape   M-50 0 L-3.2 -9.6 L50 0 L-1.6 8.5 Z   (darker tone)
upper facet   M-50 0 L-3.2 -9.6 L50 0 Z             (lighter tone)
```
Place it rotated −24° in SVG (tip up and to the right, the angle of the star's sweep). Master files: `Nova Ventures Logo Package/06 Shard/`.

**Colour pairs** (upper / lower facet), flat unless the shard is the one glass shard (below):
- Nova Blue `#1C63D3` / Deep Blue `#0F45A3`: the default
- Navy `#102A50` / Midnight `#0B1B35`
- Light Blue `#7FB3FF` / Nova Blue `#1C63D3`: on dark
- White / Pale Blue: reverse use
- Pale Blue `#DCEBFF`, flat: quiet background layers
- Hot Pink `#FF2D8A` / `#D61F72`: the accent, one supporting shard at most, never the largest

**Compositions:** individual (one shard) · directional (one dominant shard and one or two in support, large, cropped off the edge) · modular (several, ordered, in an open field) · subtle (pale flat tints behind content).

**Working rules:** up to four shards per composition (modular backgrounds excepted) · keep two shard widths clear of text, logo and trim · minimum 32 px / 10 mm long · at most one flipped shard per layout. Variety comes from scale, rotation, spacing and crop.

**Glass shard: about 1 in 10.** Glass is the exception, never the system. Across a series or campaign, roughly one shard in ten is glass; in any one layout, at most one, and every other shard stays flat.
- *Where:* only on a background that isn't white: a photograph, Navy, Midnight or the dark gradient. Never on White, and treat Cloud and Light Grey as white: on near-white surfaces glass has nothing to show through and turns into a washed-out tint, so use a flat shard there. On a flat dark surface, let it overlap a flat shard so the glass has something to show.
- *Which:* a supporting shard, never the largest. Blue only: the pink shard always stays flat.
- *Recipe:* the same base shard and facet line · upper facet Light Blue `#7FB3FF` at 34%, lower facet Nova Blue `#1C63D3` at 46% · background blur 10 px, saturation 170%, brightness 108% · 1.5 px white outline and facet line at 70%. No drop shadow, glow or gradient.
- *Placement:* clear of faces and text. One glass element per layout: a glass shard or a glass panel (section 7), never both.

**Never:** 3D, gloss, metal, glow, grain or shadow · glass or transparency outside the one glass shard above · a gradient inside a shard · new polygons, arrows or chevrons · shards arranged into a star · dense repeating tiles · a giant copy of the logo star as decoration · a shard as a UI icon, bullet or button. A layout with no graphics is fully on brand.

With photography: a shard may crop, frame or sit beside a photo, kept clear of faces. Solid by default; one supporting shard may be glass (above).

## 7. Surfaces and components

- **Surfaces are flat:** White, Light Grey, Cloud, Navy, Midnight. Separate sections by changing surface, not with shadows or boxes. Avoid Light Grey on Cloud, or two dark sections in a row.
- **Gradients are optional and quiet:** at most one per layout, on a large background only. 45° Cloud → Pale Blue, or 45° Midnight → Navy.
- **Shadows are for interfaces only** (menus, dialogs), tinted with Midnight. Never on graphics, photos, the logo or buttons.
- **Text sits on the background.** No boxed text cards. The one exception is the glass panel, over photography only (below).
- **Lines:** a 2 px Nova Blue rule above a heading or key block for emphasis; 1 px Mid Grey dividers.
- **Lists:** short items under rules, or numbered 01 02 03 in Lato Bold. No checkmarks in circles, icon bubbles or emoji.
- **Corners:** 6 px for buttons, panels and image frames · 4 px for chips · square for full-bleed photos · about 4% of the canvas width for the glass panel (40 px on a 1080 px post). Never pill shapes.
- **Buttons:** one primary per view. Primary: Midnight fill, white Lato Bold, sentence case, 6 px corners, no shadow, optional trailing arrow; Nova Blue on hover, Deep Blue when pressed. On dark: white fill with Deep Blue text. Secondary: 1 px Midnight outline. Text link: Nova Blue.
- **Glass panel:** a clear, frosted panel that holds text over a photograph.
  - *Where:* over photography only (posts, banners, stories, a web hero or menu bar over a photo). Never on a flat surface, a gradient or a shard.
  - *How many:* one per layout, and not alongside a glass shard (one glass element per layout).
  - *Recipe:* white at 24% opacity · background blur 12 px, saturation 170%, brightness 112% · 1.5 px white rim at 60% · a thin white highlight along the top edge and a soft inner glow · corners about 4% of the width. No drop shadow, no colour tint, no second gradient.
  - *Inside:* Midnight text (labels too), one phrase in Nova Blue, the pink dash, a Midnight-text call to action. The FullColour logo may sign off inside it.
  - *Placement:* keep it clear of faces, inside the 6% margin. Check that the text reads over what shows through. If the area behind is busy or dark, move the panel, raise the white to about 40%, or use a navy band instead.
  - Shards never go behind, on or inside the panel.
- **Icons:** Nova has no icon set yet. Use type, numbers and rules instead of stock line icons.

## 8. Photography

Authentic, professional and aspirational images of real people, places and possibilities connected to business in the UAE.

- **Subjects:** entrepreneurs, owners, investors and consultants in professional settings · consultations and meetings · UAE cities and business districts · modern workspaces · setup documents and procedures · life and work in the UAE.
- **Light and colour:** natural daylight, moderate contrast, neutral true-to-life colour, minimal editing. Never grade toward blue to match the palette; the brand colours sit beside the image, not on it.
- **People:** diverse (local and international), professional dress (business, smart casual or traditional), candid and mid-task, not posing at the camera unless it's a portrait. Real Nova team, office and clients wherever possible.
- **Composition:** editorial and intentional, with room for text.
- **Keep it real:** places appear as they are. Never move landmarks next to each other (for example the Burj Khalifa beside a container port).
- **Avoid:** low resolution, over-saturation, heavy blue tints or overlays, staged stock handshakes, rendered or AI-looking skylines, offices and people.
- **Text on photos:** use a calm area of the image, set it on one glass panel (section 7), or darken only the area behind the text with navy.

The photos in `images/` are generated art-direction references. Replace them with commissioned or licensed photography before public use.

## 10. Creative direction

This is direction, not a rulebook. **Start from an idea, not a layout.** Find the one thing the piece has to say, pick a way in, then design something that fits that message. Don't repeat the same layout twice in a row, and don't copy the examples in the guide.

**The feel**
- **Clear:** one idea per piece, said plainly.
- **Confident:** big type, bold crops, real space.
- **Calm:** light surfaces, natural photography, few elements.
- **Forward:** movement on the 24° sweep, from the first question to an established business.

**Ways in** (combine them, or ignore them when a better idea comes)
- **The question:** lead with what the client is actually asking, and let the type fill the frame.
- **The steps:** the journey as separate pieces coming together.
- **The person:** a real client or advisor, their own words.
- **The place:** the UAE as opportunity, photographed honestly.
- **The detail:** a close crop of something tangible, like a signed licence or office keys.
- **The fact:** one verified figure or date, set very large, with its source.

**Where to explore:** layout (type-led, photo-led, split, graphic-led or something new) · scale and contrast · cropping (full-bleed photos, shards cut off the edge, shards that frame a photo) · series rhythm (alternate light and dark, a shard travelling across carousel slides) · motion · format.

**What keeps it Nova** (the only constants): the real logo file at about 10%, with room around it · Arsenal headlines and Lato text, one highlighted phrase · palette colours, mostly light, blue as the signature · a quiet touch of Hot Pink · flat shard graphics · true, checkable content.

**Formats**

| Use | Size |
|---|---|
| Feed post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 (1:1) |
| Story, reel, short | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) |
| Banner, YouTube, LinkedIn video | 1920 × 1080 (16:9) |
| Print | A4 or A3 |

Keep text at least 6% of the short edge from every side. On 9:16, keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px, where platform UI sits.

## 11. Motion

Motion follows the same feel: calm, purposeful and forward. Everything moves along the brand's diagonal, lower-left to upper-right, and settles. Nothing bounces, spins or flashes.

**Shards**
- Slide in along their own axis (from lower-left toward upper-right), with a short fade.
- Stagger a group by 80 to 150 ms. The pink shard, if there is one, arrives last.
- Once in, they hold still, or drift very slowly (a few pixels per second) in long shots.
- Never rotate, morph, scale-pop, glow or move in 3D.

**Type**
- Headlines rise 12 to 24 px and fade in, line by line or word by word.
- The highlighted phrase arrives last.
- No typewriter effects, bounces, blur-ins or letter-by-letter scrambles.
- Hold text long enough to read twice: at least 2.5 seconds for a short line.

**Pink in motion:** the dash draws in left to right as a message starts · a marker moves to the current step · a dash on the end card signs off.

**Logo**
- Appears whole: a fade, optionally settling from 98% to 100% scale.
- Never animate the star separately, spin it, add a light sweep or flare, or build it from pieces.

**Timing and easing**

| Movement | Duration | Easing |
|---|---|---|
| Interface (hover, menus) | 150 to 250 ms | ease-out |
| Shard entrance | 900 to 1600 ms | `cubic-bezier(.16, .8, .24, 1)` |
| Text entrance | 500 to 900 ms | `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)` |
| Scene transition | 400 to 700 ms | ease-in-out |

**Transitions:** hard cuts, a shard wipe along the 24° line, or a cross-fade. No whip pans, glitches, light leaks or spinning cubes.

**End card (2 to 3 seconds):** Midnight or White background · the logo, centred or lower left, about 20% of the width (the one place it may be larger than 10%) · one line of copy or the website `[website]` · a pink dash.

**Reduced motion:** for web, respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and show the final state without animation.

## 12. Video

**Formats and lengths**

| Use | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Reels, Shorts, TikTok, stories | 9:16, 1080 × 1920 | 15 to 45 s |
| Feed video | 4:5 or 1:1 | 15 to 60 s |
| YouTube, LinkedIn, website | 16:9, 1920 × 1080 | 45 s to 3 min |

**Structure for short video:** a hook in the first 2 seconds (the client's question, said plainly) · one idea, in two to four beats · one call to action · end card.

**Footage:** real UAE places and people, natural daylight, steady and slow camera (locked-off, slow push-in, gentle slide or slow aerial). Neutral, true-to-life colour grade. People mid-task, not posing.

**Captions:** always on. Lato Bold, sentence case, white on footage with a soft navy band behind the text when needed, or Midnight on light backgrounds. Two lines at most, about 32 to 42 characters per line. Highlight one key word in Light Blue or, now and then, Hot Pink. Inside the 9:16 safe area.

**Voiceover:** clear, measured and warm. International English, unhurried (about 140 to 150 words a minute). Follows the voice rules: plain words, no hype, no false promises.

**Music:** calm, modern and minimal: soft electronic pulses, light piano or ambient textures, about 90 to 110 BPM. Sits under the voice. No epic trailer drums, no generic corporate ukulele, no tracks with lyrics under a voiceover.

**Sound:** subtle. A soft tone on the end card at most. No whooshes on every movement.

## 13. Working with tools

**The principle:** AI generates **photos and footage**. The design (logo, type, shards, layout) is **assembled from the real files**. Image and video generators can't place the logo at an exact size or render Arsenal, so they should never be asked for the finished design.

### Image generators (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Firefly and similar)
Upload this file and **one** logo PNG (the version for your background, from `Nova Ventures Logo Package/01 Horizontal/PNG/`).

The chat model writes its own short prompt for the image model, and details get lost. Ask it to include this brief word for word:

> Poster for Nova Ventures. Light, airy, editorial layout with lots of white space. Headline in a light, thin-stroked humanist sans-serif, regular weight, never bold, sentence case; one phrase in blue (#1C63D3). Body text in a clean rounded sans-serif, grey (#5B6472). Colours: white and very light grey, navy (#102A50) and Nova Blue (#1C63D3), with at most two tiny hot pink (#FF2D8A) marks: a short thin dash before the label, and the key figure. Logo small, about 10% of the width, in a corner, with nothing written beside or under it. Photo natural and unfiltered, colours true to life, no haze or wash over it. Graphics only flat, solid, slim angled blades in blue, never transparent. Numbers and short text under thin rules instead of icons. One clear call to action. A small source and date line for news.

- Upload this file, and ask for a photo or a background only, with clear space for text.
- Place the real logo and type afterwards, in a design tool or HTML.
- If you do ask for a full design for a quick concept, expect the logo and fonts to drift, and replace them before anything is published.

Photography style block (add the subject at the start):
> [Subject], editorial documentary photograph, real location in the UAE, natural daylight, neutral true-to-life colour, moderate contrast, minimal editing, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, sharp focus, realistic skin texture, people mid-task and unposed, clean uncluttered composition with open space on one side for text, calm, confident, professional mood.

### Video generators (Runway, Veo, Kling, Sora and similar)
- Generate footage and b-roll only. Add the logo, captions, shards and end card in editing.
- Ask for slow, steady camera moves and real places.

Footage style block (add the subject and camera move at the start):
> [Subject and action], [slow push-in / gentle slide / locked-off / slow aerial], real UAE location, natural daylight, neutral true-to-life colour grade, moderate contrast, steady professional cinematography, shallow depth of field, people mid-task and unposed, calm and confident pace, 24 fps, clean frame with space for captions.

### Coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and similar)
- Read this file in full.
- Build posters, decks, pages and motion in HTML, CSS and SVG, linking the real logo files and fonts. Draw shards from the geometry in section 6.
- Export stills with headless Chrome. Animate with CSS, GSAP or a video framework; follow section 11.
- Check the render against section 14 before delivering.

### Canva and Figma
- Set up a brand kit: upload Arsenal and Lato from `Fonts/`, add the palette from section 3 (including Hot Pink), and upload the logos from `Nova Ventures Logo Package/` and the shards from `06 Shard/`.
- Build templates as starting points, not fixed layouts, and vary them per section 10.
- AI features in these tools follow the same rule: photos and copy drafts, not logos or type.

## 14. Before you deliver

- Real logo file, the right version for the background, about 10% of the width, nothing beside it, no "Business beyond borders"?
- Arsenal Regular (a sans-serif, not a serif) and Lato only, with one highlighted phrase?
- Every colour from section 3, mostly light, with pink kept to one or two small marks doing different jobs?
- Shards flat, on the 24° angle, clear of text?
- At most one glass element: a panel over a photo, or a supporting blue shard on a background that isn't white? Clear of faces, with text that reads at a glance?
- One clear action, no checkmark bubbles, no boxes, no shadows, no 3D?
- Photos real, honest and unfiltered, with places shown as they are?
- Every word proofread, and every figure, date and source checked? Placeholders still marked?
- Would an international client new to UAE business setup understand it on first read?
