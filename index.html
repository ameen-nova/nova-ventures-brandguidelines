Logo system

Our logo is the most visible expression of the brand. A faceted blue-and-silver star stands for clarity, progress and global opportunity. Always use the supplied master artwork; never redraw or retype it.

The mark

A star in motion

  • 1
    Blue faceted pointsThe upper facets carry Nova Blue and Sky Blue: confidence and direction.
  • 2
    Silver sweepThe lower arc moves from lower-left to upper-right, the same forward direction used by the shard.
  • 3
    Dimensional planesLight and shadow across the facets create depth. That rendering belongs to the logo alone; everything around it stays flat.

Logo hierarchy

Signature for showpieces, flat for everyday

The artwork is fixed; what we decide is which version leads. The dimensional logo is the signature and appears where it has room to shine. The flat star carries the everyday, small and single-colour work, so the brand never depends on rendering to look right.

1 · Signature · DimensionalCovers, signage, website hero, video, title slides and event backdrops, at generous sizes. The only place metallic shading appears.
2 · Everyday · Flat Nova BlueInterface, icons, avatars, documents, forms and anywhere the symbol appears small or alone. Clean at every size.
3 · Single colour · Flat white or blackFoil, embossing, stamps, embroidery, engraving and one-colour print.

Flat Nova Blue is the client’s own flat star filled with Nova Blue; nothing is redrawn.

Protecting the artwork

Three rules this logo needs

The logo’s fine silver shading and thin wordmark are part of its character. These rules keep them visible.

Small sizes

Use the full-colour symbol from 48 px up. For favicons and app icons, use the pixel-tuned files in 05 Digital, never a scaled-down logo. For single-colour work under 48 px (stamps, embroidery, foil), use the Flat symbol.

White backgrounds

The silver sweep is pale (1.8:1 against white). On pure white keep the horizontal logo at least 170 px or 40 mm wide. For smaller placements, put Cloud or Light Grey behind it.

The only metallic element

The dimensional logo is the one place metallic shading appears, and it is reserved for signature moments. The shard, icons and interface stay flat, so the logo keeps its shine. Never add bevels, glows or shadows to it.

Colour

Deep blues, silver and clean neutrals, sampled directly from the logo artwork. Blue gives confidence and direction, silver adds precision, white creates clarity and charcoal carries authority.

Primary colours

The core of the identity

Balance and readability

Colour proportions

Mostly light, anchored by navy, signed in blue

Nova Blue is the signature, not the backdrop: the star, buttons, links and one highlight per headline. Navy and charcoal carry authority. Deep Blue, Sky Blue and Silver are supporting tones for hover states, charts and shard facets, never the main colour of a layout. Dark applications can invert the balance, with navy dominant.

Typography rules

  • Set headlines in Arsenal Regular, in sentence case, with tight line spacing (1.03–1.18). Never all caps, never bold.
  • Use Arsenal at 28 px and above only. Smaller headings use Lato Bold.
  • Highlight one key phrase per headline in Nova Blue. Never more than one.
  • Body and lead text in Lato Regular, Slate or Charcoal, 16 px minimum on screen, 50–75 characters per line.
  • Buttons: Lato Bold, 6 px corners, one primary per view. Full spec in Buttons below.
  • Left-align text. Centre only short, single statements.
  • Where the brand fonts are unavailable (e.g. Outlook), use Georgia for headlines and Arial for text.

05

Depth & surfaces

Depth comes from composition, not rendering: overlap, scale, white space, tone and real photography. Surfaces are flat by default. The dimensional logo is the only rendered element in the system; everything around it stays flat, so the logo keeps its presence.

Where depth comes from

Four ways to build depth without effects

If an element needs gradients and shadows to look good, the layout needs work. Reach for these first.

OverlapTwo flat shards in different tones, one over the other.
ScaleA large cropped shard against a small one.
SpaceGenerous white space around a single element.
ToneLight and dark surfaces set against each other.

Gradients

Two quiet gradients, both optional

Gradients are a background tone, never a graphic. Use at most one per layout, only on large surfaces, and never inside a shard, on the logo or behind small text on Light Blue.

Light backgroundHero sections, covers and large light panels.

Dark backgroundCovers, footers and dark presentation slides.

45° · Midnight #0B1B35 → Navy #102A50

Two colours, adjacent in the palette, on the brand diagonal (45°, lower-left to upper-right). No bright blue, no silver and no third stop.

Shadows

For interfaces only

Shadows show that something sits above the page: menus, dialogs, inputs and cards that lift on hover. Buttons stay flat. Graphics, shards, photography and the logo never get a shadow. Shadows are tinted with Midnight, never pure black.

One quiet gradient on a large background.

  • ✓Let photography bring natural depth and texture.

✕Don’t

Use rendered blades, light effects or grain.

  • ✕Add bevels, metallic shine, glass or glow to anything but the logo artwork.
  • ✕Stack gradients, or use more than one in a layout.
06

Brand graphics: The Shard System

Nova Ventures’ graphic language is built from one element drawn from the star. The shard stands for the individual elements of a business journey: formation, licensing, visas, compliance and ongoing support. Brought together through controlled arrangement, overlap, rotation and cropping, they form one structured whole and move in one clear direction.

The Shard System

Different elements. One clear direction.

Core principles

What the shards do, and why it matters to Nova

A flexible graphic language built from one distinctive element, where individual forms come together to create a coherent whole.

01 · Structure

Individual shards are distinct elements; their arrangement creates one organised composition.

Nova brings separate business requirements together through a structured approach.

02 · Direction

The asymmetrical, tapered form introduces movement and progression, without becoming an arrow.

Nova moves clients from first requirements to an established business.

03 · Connection

Shards interact through proximity, alignment and controlled overlap.

Services, people and processes work together toward one business objective.

Point of origin

From the star, to the shard, to the system

The shard is not invented decoration. It takes the angle and taper of the star’s sweep, so the wider visual language always leads back to the logo the client chose.

The starThe overarching identity.
The shardOne element, drawn from the star.
The systemMany shards, one flexible composition.

Construction

Built on the star’s sweep

The base shard sits on the same 24° line as the star’s sweep, tip to tip. Every shard in the system is this one shape: never redrawn, stretched or given extra facets.

Anchor points
Four: two tips and two shoulders, set just off-centre so the form is asymmetrical.
Facets
Split tip to tip. The upper facet is the lighter tone, the lower facet one step darker.
Proportion
Length 100 : width 18, fixed.
Orientation
Tip up and to the right at 24°, the angle of the star’s sweep.

Master files: Nova Ventures Logo Package/06 Shard. Build every composition from these files, never from a traced or redrawn shape.

Asset hierarchy

The logo leads, the shard supports

  1. Primary identity: the Nova Ventures logo.
  2. Secondary graphic asset: the shard.
  3. Graphic compositions: controlled arrangements of the base shard.
  4. Interface icons: a separate icon system. The shard never stands in for a UI icon, button or status marker.

Variations

Five controlled uses of one shape

Every variation comes from the same base shard. A variation is a way of placing the shard, not permission to invent a new shape.

PrimaryThe base shard on the sweep angle. Upper facet light, lower facet dark.
RotatedTurned to suit a composition, never to point at content like an arrow.
FlippedMirrored for balance; the dark facet moves to the top. Use sparingly.
CroppedRunning off the edge with enough of the form left to read.
ExtendedTwo shards on one line with a controlled overlap, as a divider or accent.

Colour

Two flat facets, one tone apart

Each shard pairs two neighbouring palette tones split on the facet line, which gives depth without rendering. Pale tints are single flat colours for quiet background layers. Never a gradient inside a shard.

Nova BlueNova Blue · Deep Blue facet. Default
NavyNavy · Midnight facet
Light BlueLight Blue · Nova Blue facet. On dark
Pale tintPale Blue, flat. Quiet background layers
WhiteWhite · Pale Blue facet. Reverse use

How it behaves

Four compositions, each with a job

Fewer shapes, stronger rules. Every composition uses the same base shard; variety comes only from scale, rotation, spacing, cropping and arrangement. Never from new shapes, gradient bands or dense repeating tiles.

The individual elementOne shard: a single service, decision or opportunity.

Small accents, section markers, simple layouts

The modular compositionSeveral shards on one angle in an ordered, open field: separate elements forming one structure.

Brand backgrounds, presentation covers, selected campaigns

The directional compositionOne dominant shard with one or two in support, moving the eye forward.

Website heroes, brochure covers, large format

The subtle compositionPale flat tints, low contrast, so the content stays dominant.

Supporting backgrounds behind content areas

With photography

Frame the photo, don’t filter it

  • The shard may crop, frame or sit beside a photograph.
  • Keep it clear of faces and key information in the image.
  • Use solid shapes, never translucent or metallic overlays.
  • Let the photograph keep its natural colour and depth.

On the web

Calm, clear, information-led

  • White and neutral surfaces, navy type, blue for actions.
  • Subtle borders, minimal shadows.
  • The shard is a section-level or editorial accent, not interface decoration.
  • Never turn it into a button, bullet, status icon or navigation marker.

✓Do

  • ✓Build every shard from the base shard.
  • ✓Keep the slim, asymmetrical silhouette and fixed proportions.
  • ✓Use flat palette tones, split on the facet line.
  • ✓Crop with intention and leave generous negative space.
  • ✓Use it selectively, to support the content.

✕Don’t

  • ✕Invent new polygons or turn the shard into an arrow or chevron.
  • ✕Add gradients, metallic, glass, glow, grain or shadow.
  • ✕Stretch, distort or crop it beyond recognition.
  • ✕Arrange shards into a star, scatter them randomly, or pack them into dense tiles that read as a sports or tech brand.
  • ✕Put it in every card and section, or use it in place of the logo.

Voice & messaging

Nova Ventures sounds like an expert who makes things clear: knowledgeable, professional, concise, reassuring and action-oriented.

Core communication principle

Clarity builds confidence.

We explain complex business setup processes in simple, professional language, so clients understand their options, know what comes next and move forward with confidence.

Tone attributes

Voice pillars

Four qualities, each with a limit

Clear, never complicated

Familiar words, short sentences, direct explanations. Explain unfamiliar terms.

Say“Choose the right business setup for your needs.”
Avoid“Optimise your jurisdictional incorporation strategy.”

Confident, never boastful

Show expertise through useful information and claims we can support.

Say“Our team will guide you through the setup process.”
Avoid“We are the undisputed number one business consultants in the UAE.”

Supportive, never overfamiliar

Professional and approachable, without slang or excessive friendliness.

Say“We’re here to help you understand your options.”
Avoid“Hey! Let’s get your dream business sorted, buddy!”

Solution-focused, never pushy

Outcomes and practical next steps, not pressure or exaggerated promises.

Say“Explore the package that suits your business.”
Avoid“Sign up today or miss your only chance!”

Writing rules

Five habits

Be direct
One main idea per sentence. Active voice. Say what happens next.
Benefit first
Explain what a service helps the client achieve before the technical detail.
Explain terms
Use “free zone”, “trade licence” and “residency visa” where needed, and explain them.
Next steps
What the client provides, what Nova handles, and what depends on an authority.
No false promises
No guaranteed approvals, fixed timings, universal eligibility or “lowest price” claims.
